Playxera Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Playxera. Playxera is a sports platform that helps users discover sports grounds, academies, coaches, matches, tournaments and sports products. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Playxera may collect:

  • Name and profile information.
  • Email address and mobile number.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Profile photo, if you choose to provide one.
  • Sports interests, match history and performance statistics.
  • Booking, registration, order and transaction records.
  • Location information when required for nearby grounds, academies, coaches or directions.
  • Information you submit through forms, reviews or support requests.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the app.

3. Google Sign-In and Authentication

Playxera may offer Google Sign-In or other authentication methods. When you use Google Sign-In, information made available through the authentication process, such as your name, email address and profile photo, may be used to create or manage your account. Google's handling of your information is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

4. Location Information

With your permission, Playxera may access your device location to help you find nearby sports facilities and relevant services. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. If you deny location permission, some location-based features may not work as intended.

5. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Enable ground, academy and coaching discovery.
  • Process bookings, match registrations and product orders.
  • Maintain sports statistics, match history and achievements.
  • Manage payments, refunds and wallet-related records.
  • Send important account, booking and service notifications.
  • Provide customer support and improve app performance.
  • Detect fraud, prevent misuse and maintain platform security.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

6. Payments and Wallet

If Playxera supports payments, wallet balances, refunds or withdrawals, relevant transaction information may be processed to provide these services. Payment information may be handled by third-party payment providers. We do not claim to store complete card details unless this is actually implemented and appropriately secured.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information as part of our stated privacy practices. Information may be shared when necessary with service providers, authentication providers, payment processors, relevant booking or event participants, authorized platform administrators, or legal authorities where required by law.

For example, information needed to manage a booking may be shared with the relevant ground or academy. Information visible on public profiles, match results or leaderboards may be accessible to other users depending on the app's features and privacy settings.

8. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Such measures may include access controls, secure communication and appropriate technical safeguards. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes and comply with applicable laws.

Users may request account deletion and deletion of associated personal information, subject to applicable legal obligations and any records that must lawfully be retained. Where account deletion is available in the app, users may use that feature. Otherwise, users can contact us using the contact details provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

Playxera is not intended to collect children's personal information unlawfully. Where parental consent or other safeguards are required by applicable law, we will follow those requirements. A parent or guardian may contact us regarding a child's information.

11. Third-Party Services

Playxera may use third-party services such as Google Sign-In, mapping services, hosting providers and payment processors. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Your Privacy Choices
